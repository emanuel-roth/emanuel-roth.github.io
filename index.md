<div id="describe-text">
	<p>My masters thesis was on canonical reductions of principal bundles, which is now a <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.17371">preprint</a> with Florent Schaffhauser. It generalizes Harder-Narasimhan filtrations to symplectic and orthogonal bundles. My bachelors thesis was on <a href="/assets/bachelor.pdf">the Mitchell embedding theorem</a>.</p>
